## Why

Learners need a way to see note lengths against the beat. Pre-readers need a practice flow they can follow through shapes, color, and sound.

## What Changes

- Show a visual beat pulse, moving playhead, and duration bars.
- Offer an optional simplified Kids mode with separate progress.
- Show authored fingering in lessons 1–15 with matching hand and key markers.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `practice`: Visual rhythm, Kids mode, and fingering.
- `progress-and-delivery`: Independent practice progress for the two modes.

## Impact

The practice studio and keyboard gain visual cues. Kids mode saves a second local progress record.
