## Why

Wide two-hand passages can put required keys off-screen on a phone. The key-hint toggle currently helps in guided practice only.

## What Changes

- Fit a wider pitch range on narrow screens in two-hand lessons.
- Follow the selected score passage with optional key hints in all practice modes.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `instrument-input`: Compact touch keyboard for two-hand lessons.
- `practice`: Synchronized key hints in guided and timed practice and demonstrations.

## Impact

The browser keyboard layout and its practice highlighting change.
