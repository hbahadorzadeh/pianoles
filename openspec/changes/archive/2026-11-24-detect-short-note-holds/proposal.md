## Why

A learner can lift a key early and still play the next note on beat. Onset feedback alone misses the short hold.

## What Changes

Track the time from press to release for each matched pitch. Show an independent early-release cue on the bar and name the note in the feedback.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `practice`: Separate note-hold feedback from onset timing.

## Impact

Guided and timed practice display feedback for short holds. Pitch and onset scores keep their existing definitions.
