## Context

The app already reads optional fingering from YAML and shows the authored fingers during play.

## Goals / Non-Goals

Fill the missing guidance in lessons 48–86 and give learners a focused progression for each clef.

## Decisions

- Assign fingers per written pitch, using hand-position changes and chord fingerings suited to the exercises.
- Teach clef landmarks, bottom-to-top lines and spaces, and mixed reading in separate right- and left-hand exercises.
- Keep key hints optional so learners can test their reading directly on the score.
