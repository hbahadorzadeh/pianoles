## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Suggested fingering
The app SHALL show authored finger suggestions where present and provide them for every note in lessons 48–86 with whole hand diagrams that move beneath the target keys, with matching colors on the active fingertips and keys.

#### Scenario: Use the right thumb for middle C
- **WHEN** the learner starts guided practice in lesson 1 with finger hints enabled
- **THEN** the right hand moves below middle C and its thumb matches the accent on the key

#### Scenario: Practice a later two-hand chord
- **WHEN** a learner reaches a chord in lessons 48–86 with finger hints enabled
- **THEN** the app shows all suggested fingers on the corresponding hands and keys
