## Why

Learners want a larger course with recognizable pieces. Contributors need a way to author lessons independently of the application code. Sustain studies need a written cue and a visible control.

## What Changes

- Add 50 lessons and migrate all 86 to individual YAML files.
- Document contributor syntax and validate the files during builds.
- Add written sustain cues, a pedal display and control, and MIDI sustain handling.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `lesson-catalog`: Contributor-friendly YAML catalog and an expanded course.
- `practice`: Written and visible sustain guidance.
- `instrument-input`: On-screen and MIDI sustain.

## Impact

Lesson files carry notes, fingerings, metadata, and optional pedal cues. Published lesson IDs and local progress remain compatible.
