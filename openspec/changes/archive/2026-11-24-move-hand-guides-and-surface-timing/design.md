## Context

The keyboard already has authored beginner fingerings and the timed engine already reports onset timing.

## Goals / Non-Goals

Make both cues visible without changing guided pitch scoring or standard timing scores.

## Decisions

- Move each whole hand to its suggested pitches, maintaining separation and respecting reduced motion. Match its colored fingertips with accents at the bottom of the target keys.
- Fill the guided note bars over their written durations at the selected tempo. Keep guided input self-paced.
- Add a clock button for timed practice in Kids mode. Reuse the existing early, on-beat, and late feedback.
