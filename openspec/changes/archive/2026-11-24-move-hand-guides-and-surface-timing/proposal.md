## Why

Learners should see the hand move to the key it will play. Duration and timing cues should be easy to find in standard and Kids modes.

## What Changes

Move the whole hand guide beneath the required keys, fill the guided duration bar, and expose Timed practice in Kids mode.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `practice`: Moving hand guides and accessible rhythm practice.

## Impact

The guided timeline and keyboard change. Kids mode gains a clock control for timed practice.
