## Why

Music theory needs a separate, keyboard-free lesson format. Household members need independent progress on a shared browser.

## What Changes

- Create a Music theory category with notation examples, listening exercises, and quizzes.
- Let learners create and switch between named local profiles.
- Migrate progress from the earlier standard and Kids mode records.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `lesson-catalog`: Theory lessons and validated YAML content.
- `progress-and-delivery`: Profile-specific lesson history, scores, and view preferences.

## Impact

Lessons 87 and 88 retain their URLs and become theory lessons. Lessons 89 and 90 cover duration and meter. Progress remains in browser storage.
