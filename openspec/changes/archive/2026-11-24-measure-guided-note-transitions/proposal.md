## Why

Guided practice should wait for the learner's first correct input and then show whether they switch to the next note too early or late.

## What Changes

Start each guided duration bar on the accepted input. Color the preceding bar on the next accepted input, using soft blue for early and muted red for late.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `practice`: Guided bar starts and transition timing feedback.

## Impact

Guided rhythm feedback becomes available in standard and Kids modes. Existing scoring remains unchanged.
