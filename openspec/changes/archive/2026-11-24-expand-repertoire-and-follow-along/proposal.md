## Why

Learners want recognizable public-domain music and a way to follow the lesson tempo without a MIDI connection.

## What Changes

Add 12 credited arrangements from familiar melodies through two-hand classical studies. Add a five-second countdown into follow-along playback when Guided starts without an open MIDI input.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `lesson-catalog`: Expanded public-domain repertoire and access in Kids mode.
- `practice`: Tempo-based follow-along when MIDI is unavailable.

## Impact

The course grows to 36 lessons and uses catalog-derived totals. Existing lesson numbers and saved progress remain compatible.
