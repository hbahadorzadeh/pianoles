## Why

Kids need to connect the highlighted key to written notation. A numbered badge on the key and a moving cursor alone leave the expected finger and note length less clear.

## What Changes

- Keep a focused score visible in Kids mode.
- Put a matching fingertip shape at the bottom of the suggested key.
- Fill note and rest bars at the current tempo and show early and late feedback in timed practice.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `practice`: Pre-reader notation, fingering, and visual timing feedback.

## Impact

The score, piano hints, and rhythm panel change. Timed practice keeps its existing scoring.
